Senior Data Engineer - Fintech Analytics (Remote, LATAM)

We are a fictional fintech company building the analytics platform behind our payments products. We are looking for a Data Engineer to design, build and maintain our data pipelines and warehouse.

Responsibilities:
- Design and maintain ELT/ETL pipelines on Google Cloud Platform.
- Model data in BigQuery using dimensional modeling (star and snowflake schemas).
- Build modular transformations with dbt and orchestrate them with Apache Airflow.
- Work with business teams to deliver financial KPIs in Power BI or Looker.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience as a Data Engineer or in a similar role.
- Strong Python and SQL skills.
- Hands-on experience with BigQuery, dbt and Airflow.
- Experience with Git and CI practices.
- Fluent English, written and spoken.

Nice to have:
- Google Cloud Professional Data Engineer certification.
- Experience with Docker and Terraform.
- Experience using AI tools such as GitHub Copilot.
